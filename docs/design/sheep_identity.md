# Sheep identity

One sheep combines stable identity with independent dimensions: genome, derived phenotype, persistent personality axes, food preferences, flat ancestry and derived relationships, append-only history, explicit legacy tags, and stable-ID breeder notes. Similar genomes can therefore yield distinct preferences, temperament, history, and player meaning.

`SheepRecord` contains persistent data only. Focused resolvers generate traits, bonding tendencies, and elder affinity; services manage history, notes, statistics, breeding, and pedigree. No movement, AI, feeding, bonding, animation, or life-stage simulation is implemented.
