## Phenotype summaries and carriers

- **Clover:** black points, white neutral wool, soft waves, fluffy medium fleece, petite/standard build, side medium ears, no horns. Carries brown points and a hidden point-pattern allele.
- **Biscuit:** chocolate points, warm fawn wool, soft waves, fluffy medium fleece, standard build, floppy medium ears, no horns. Carries point dilution and lavender.
- **Poppy:** silver points, neutral fawn wool, cloud medium fleece, expressed patches and speckles, standard body, side ears, no horns. Carries wool dilution and horn presence.
- **Bean:** black points, diluted taupe wool, straight fluffy medium fleece, expressed mask and blaze, round body, side ears, no horns, small star. Carries brown points.
