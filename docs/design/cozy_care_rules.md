# Cozy care rules

1. Sheep do not die.
2. Sheep do not become permanently harmed through neglect.
3. There are no real-world absence penalties or wall-clock care decay.
4. There are no login or daily streaks.
5. Archived sheep are completely frozen.
6. Poor care creates only recoverable temporary consequences.
7. Positive interaction is generally more important than punishment.

Simulation advances only when authoritative game time advances. Neglect never changes genome, phenotype, personality, pedigree, bond, or permanent identity and never creates disease, injury, loss, or deletion.
