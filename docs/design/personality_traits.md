# Personality traits

Traits are deterministic labels derived from persistent axes and may coexist.

- **CALM:** high calmness.
- **SOCIAL:** high sociability.
- **SHY:** low boldness.
- **GREEDY:** high food drive.
- **CURIOUS:** high curiosity.
- **INDEPENDENT:** low sociability and at least moderate boldness.
- **CUDDLY:** high affection.
- **MISCHIEVOUS:** high mischief.
- **SLEEPY:** low energy.
- **ZOOMY:** high energy and boldness.

These labels are future behavior inputs, not AI or animation state.
